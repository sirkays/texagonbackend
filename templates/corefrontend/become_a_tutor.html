Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need formal teaching credentials?

No, formal teaching credentials are not required. What matters is your expertise in your chosen field and your ability to communicate complex concepts clearly to secondary school students.

How much does a tutor earn?

Compensation is based on your experience level, expertise, and availability. Full-time tutors earn competitive rates with opportunities for bonuses based on student performance. Contact us for detailed compensation information.

Can I teach multiple specializations?

Absolutely! If you have expertise in multiple areas, you can specialize in all of them. This gives you more teaching opportunities and helps us better serve our student population.

What kind of support do tutors receive?

We provide comprehensive support including curriculum resources, training materials, a dedicated support team, access to other tutors, and regular feedback on your teaching performance.

What's the selection process timeline?

Our selection process typically takes 1-2 weeks from initial application to onboarding. We review applications regularly and aim to get back to candidates within 2-3 business days.
